# Human-LLM Division of Labor

**Summary**: The collaborative balance in an LLM Wiki where humans direct curation and synthesis while LLMs perform the structural maintenance and bookkeeping.

**Sources**: `raw/index.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-08-16

---

## Why Human-Maintained Wikis Fail

Traditional wikis and personal knowledge management systems (PKMs) frequently fail due to **maintenance fatigue** (source: index.md). As a knowledge base expands:
- Updating backlinks across dozens of related notes becomes tedious (source: index.md).
- Re-evaluating older notes against newly ingested documents requires excessive manual effort (source: index.md).
- The bookkeeping burden eventually outgrows the perceived value, leading to abandoned wikis (source: index.md).

---

## The Asymmetric Collaboration Model

The LLM Wiki pattern assigns responsibilities based on comparative strengths (source: index.md):

| Dimension | Human Role | LLM Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sourcing** | Curates high-quality raw materials (articles, papers, notes) | Ingests and extracts key insights |
| **Direction** | Chooses what to investigate, question, and emphasize | Follows instructions and provides structured answers |
| **Maintenance** | Reads summaries and navigates the graph view | Maintains cross-links, indexes, and logs across files |
| **Bookkeeping** | Zero manual backlinking required | Modifies 10–15 files in a single pass without fatigue |
| **Synthesis** | Evaluates strategic conclusions and high-level ideas | Resolves contradictions and synthesizes claims across files |

By dropping the marginal cost of structural maintenance near zero, the knowledge base remains perpetually clean, organized, and up to date (source: index.md).
